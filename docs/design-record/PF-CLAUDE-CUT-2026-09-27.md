Four readers compared PF's `CLAUDE.md` (2,764 lines, 281 KB, at PF's head `d8c895fc`) passage by passage
with the rulebook PF loads at GBGCodingTools v2.0.0-rc.30 (`.gbg/policy/*.md`, imported by the block at
the head of that same file). Every PF agent loads both, so a rule restated in `CLAUDE.md` is paid for
twice in every context and drifts from the rule it copies.

Each passage is marked:

- `DUPLICATE` — says what the named rule says, and nothing a PF session would lose: delete it.
- `TRIM` — part restates the rule (**goes**), part is PF's own (**stays**): cut the first, keep the second.
- `CONFLICT` — says something the rule contradicts. Not for the session to settle: each is a question for
  the owner (keep PF's version as a switch or setting in `.gbg/config.jl`, or drop it for the rule).
- `kept` — PF's own: its design, decisions, state, science and plans. Untouched.

Line numbers are those of `d8c895fc`; every marked passage quotes its first words, so it can be found
after the numbers move. The list is a measurement by readers, not a ruling: the PF session checks each
item against the text before acting on it.
